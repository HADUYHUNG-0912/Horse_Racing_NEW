\section{Product Background}

In the context of Vietnam's growing interest in sports entertainment and digital transformation, horse racing organizations are increasingly facing the need to manage tournaments, participants, horses, jockeys, and race registration processes more efficiently. However, many racing-related activities may still depend on manual management methods such as paper forms, scattered Excel files, direct communication, or disconnected systems. This can lead to difficulties in managing tournament information, horse and jockey profiles, owner registrations, approval status, and communication between horse owners, jockeys, referees, and administrators.

Recognizing these limitations, a student team has proposed the development of a Horse Racing Management System. This system aims to digitize the main workflow of horse racing event management, from tournament creation and participant registration to approval, race organization, and result tracking. The target users include horse owners who register horses for tournaments, jockeys who participate in races, administrators who manage tournaments and registration requests, referees who monitor race results, and viewers or related users who need to follow tournament information in a transparent and convenient way.


\section{Existing Systems}

\subsection{Prism Horse}

\textbf{Link:} \url{https://prism.horse}

\textbf{Description:} A horse racing management platform supporting race events, horse information, and participant management.

\textbf{Actors:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Horse Owners
    \item Jockeys
    \item Administrators
    \item Referees
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Features:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Horse and jockey management.
    \item Tournament organization.
    \item Race registration.
    \item Participant management.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Pros:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Designed specifically for horse racing activities.
    \item Supports race and participant management.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Cons:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Limited owner--jockey collaboration workflow.
    \item No invitation management mechanism.
    \item Limited registration status tracking.
    \item Lack of integrated communication features.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{RaceDB}

\textbf{Link:} \url{https://www.racedb.com}

\textbf{Description:} A race event management system that supports competition registration, participant management, race scheduling, and result tracking. The platform is primarily designed for cycling and sporting competitions.

\textbf{Actors:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Participants
    \item Event Organizers
    \item Administrators
    \item Race Officials
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Features:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Online participant registration.
    \item Race scheduling and event management.
    \item Result recording and ranking.
    \item Participant and competition tracking.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Pros:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Supports online registration and race management.
    \item Provides race scheduling and result tracking.
    \item Offers a centralized system for competition administration.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Cons:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Not specifically designed for horse racing.
    \item Does not support horse owner, horse, and jockey relationships.
    \item Lacks invitation and approval workflows between horse owners and jockeys.
    \item Does not provide horse profile management and ownership information.
    \item Limited support for horse racing officials and tournament-specific workflows.
\end{itemize}
