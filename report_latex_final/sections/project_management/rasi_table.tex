\section{Responsibility Assignment Matrix (DRSI)}

To clarify roles and responsibilities across the team, the project utilizes a DRSI matrix (Do, Review, Support, Informed). The abbreviations used in the matrix are defined as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{D (Do)}: The member responsible for developing and implementing the deliverable.
    \item \textbf{R (Review)}: The member responsible for reviewing and approving the quality of the work.
    \item \textbf{S (Support)}: The member providing support during development or integration.
    \item \textbf{I (Informed)}: Members kept updated on the status of the deliverable.
    \item \textbf{- (Omitted)}: Member has no direct responsibility for the deliverable.
\end{itemize}
