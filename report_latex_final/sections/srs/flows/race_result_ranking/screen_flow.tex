\subsubsection{Referee Screen Description}

\TableStart{|p{0.05\textwidth}|p{0.18\textwidth}|p{0.20\textwidth}|p{0.47\textwidth}|}
\textbf{\#} & \textbf{Feature} & \textbf{Screen} & \textbf{Description} \\
\hline
1 & General & Login & Authenticate the referee and enter the Referee Dashboard. \\
\hline
2 & Dashboard & Referee Dashboard & Main hub for the referee with navigation to assigned races, results management, and profile. \\
\hline
3 & Race Management & Assigned Race List & Display all races assigned to the referee, including tournament, date, venue, and current status. \\
\hline
4 & Race Management & Race Detail & Show detailed information of the selected race, including participating horses, jockeys, and schedule. \\
\hline
5 & Result Entry & Input Result Form & Allow the referee to enter finishing positions, times, and relevant notes for each horse in the race. \\
\hline
6 & Result Entry & Result Preview & Display a summary of the entered results for the referee to review before submission. \\
\hline
7 & Result Submission & Pending Result & Hold the submitted result in a pending state awaiting approval from the administrator. \\
\hline
8 & Result Review & Admin Review & Administrator reviews the submitted result and either confirms or rejects it. \\
\hline
9 & Result Correction & Edit Result & Allow the referee to correct and update the result after it has been rejected by the administrator. \\
\hline
10 & Ranking & Ranking Board & Display the official race ranking after the result has been confirmed by the administrator. \\
\hline
11 & Ranking & Leaderboard & Show the overall tournament leaderboard updated with the latest confirmed race results. \\
\TableFinish{Referee screen descriptions}{tab:referee_screen_screens}
