\subsection{Business Rules for Admin Role}

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{|l|p{12cm}|}
        \hline
        \textbf{ID} & \textbf{Rule Definition} \\
        \hline
        BR-01 & Only an authenticated Administrator with the ADMIN role may access the admin management screens and perform administrative operations. \\
        \hline
        BR-02 & The Administrator must create or update a tournament only when all required information, including name, dates, location, and round structure, has been provided. \\
        \hline
        BR-03 & A tournament cannot be deleted while it still contains active races, registered participants, or reward definitions that depend on it. \\
        \hline
        BR-04 & Every race must be associated with an existing tournament round, and its scheduled date and time must remain within the valid round timeframe. \\
        \hline
        BR-05 & The Administrator must assign a unique lane number to each horse-jockey pair within the same race heat. \\
        \hline
        BR-06 & The system must prevent the Administrator from creating duplicate lane assignments or scheduling a horse into overlapping races. \\
        \hline
        BR-07 & A referee may be assigned to a race only when the referee is available and has no conflicting assignment within the allowed time window. \\
        \hline
        BR-08 & The Administrator may define rewards only for an existing tournament, and prize ranks must be unique within that tournament. \\
        \hline
        BR-09 & User account management by the Administrator must validate role assignment and allow create, update, lock, or delete actions only for valid account records. \\
        \hline
        BR-10 & The system must reject invalid administrative input and display a clear error message when tournament, race, reward, or user data is incomplete or inconsistent. \\
        \hline
        BR-11 & A successful create, update, or delete action by the Administrator must immediately refresh the relevant list or dashboard so the latest system state is visible. \\
        \hline
        BR-12 & The Administrator must be able to review current tournament status, race schedules, rewards, and user records from the admin overview and management screens. \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
    \caption{Business Rules for Admin Role in Horse Racing Management System}
    \label{tab:admin_business_rules}
\end{table}

\subsection{Business Rules for Horse Owner Role}

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{|l|p{12cm}|}
        \hline
        \textbf{ID} & \textbf{Rule Definition} \\
        \hline
        BR-01 & Only an authenticated Horse Owner with the OWNER role may access the owner dashboard and perform owner-related operations. \\
        \hline
        BR-02 & The Horse Owner may register a horse only when all required horse information, including name, age, breed, and gender, has been provided. \\
        \hline
        BR-03 & A horse record may be updated or deleted only by its owner and only when the horse is not currently involved in an active or locked registration. \\
        \hline
        BR-04 & The Horse Owner may send a jockey invitation only when both the selected jockey and the selected horse are valid and belong to an eligible tournament context. \\
        \hline
        BR-05 & A jockey invitation must contain valid invitation details and may be accepted or rejected by the recipient before it can be used for tournament registration. \\
        \hline
        BR-06 & Tournament registration is allowed only when the Horse Owner has an accepted jockey invitation and the selected horse-jockey pair is valid for the chosen tournament. \\
        \hline
        BR-07 & The system must prevent duplicate tournament registrations for the same horse-jockey pair in the same tournament. \\
        \hline
        BR-08 & The Horse Owner may view the current registration status and approval result of each submitted registration request from the registered tournaments screen. \\
        \hline
        BR-09 & The Horse Owner may view upcoming race schedules and completed race results for owned horses, including rankings, scores, remarks, and violations. \\
        \hline
        BR-10 & The Horse Owner may update personal profile information, including contact and organization details, only after valid input is provided and saved successfully. \\
        \hline
        BR-11 & The system must display clear success or error messages after each owner action, such as horse registration, invitation sending, registration submission, or profile update. \\
        \hline
        BR-12 & The Horse Owner must be able to view rankings and performance summaries for horses and jockeys through the ranking page. \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
    \caption{Business Rules for Horse Owner Role in Horse Racing Management System}
    \label{tab:horse_owner_business_rules}
\end{table}
